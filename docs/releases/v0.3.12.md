# LocalDictate v0.3.12 — chained meeting recordings, visible notification, bubble ✕ fixed

## Meeting

- **Chain recordings** (« Enchaîner les enregistrements » in « Réglages réunion », **off by default**): when a recording reaches its 2-hour limit, it is closed and a new one starts at once. Each part is its own meeting (« Partie 2 (suite automatique) »…) and is transcribed on its own. A fraction of a second may be missing between two parts. The chain stops on « Arrêter l’enregistrement », on any error, or when the storage or the 20-meeting limit is reached (with an alert).
- **Visible notification while recording**: a microphone icon in the status bar, « ● Réunion en cours d’enregistrement » with the elapsed time and an « Arrêter l’enregistrement » button. Tapping it opens the app on the « Réunion » tab.

## Bubble

- **Dropping the bubble on the ✕ hides it again.** The app compared the bubble with a ✕ position computed for the whole screen, while the ✕ is drawn above the navigation bar, so the drop was missed on the phone. It now compares the finger with the ✕ as drawn.

Checked by host tests only; to be confirmed on the phone.

## Installation

Installs over the previous version and keeps the models, recordings, history, statistics and « Mes mots ».
