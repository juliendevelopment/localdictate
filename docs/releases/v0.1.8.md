# LocalDictate v0.1.8 — reliable insertion, redesign

## Why

Versions 0.1.0 to 0.1.7 required that no event or state change between « Dicter » and « Arrêter ». On the phone, the keyboard, the bubble, the notification and Keep change during a dictation: each version lifted one refusal and revealed another. Full analysis: `docs/insertion.md`.

## Changes

- One decision at stop time, on fresh reads: same focused field, editor acquired at that moment, matching text and cursor. Accessibility events decide nothing anymore; input restarted on the same field is accepted.
- Nothing is replaced: selected text blocks insertion; a word being typed in Gboard stays intact and the dictation is inserted next to it.
- Every dictation is saved in the last 5 texts **before** insertion. If insertion is not confirmed: automatic copy to the clipboard, and the bubble goes straight back to « ● Dicter » with the notice « Non inséré • texte copié, collez-le ».
- Refusal messages reworded, still without field content.

## Installation

Same procedure as v0.1.0; installs over the previous version and keeps the models. To check on the phone: several dictations in a row in Keep without touching the screen, a 5-minute dictation, another app.
