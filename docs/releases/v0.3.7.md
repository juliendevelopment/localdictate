# LocalDictate v0.3.7 — soft speech picked up in dictation

## Change

- Speaking softly close to the microphone was often missed in dictation (bubble), while a loud voice worked. The microphone source used for recognition (`VOICE_RECOGNITION`) applies no gain, so soft speech reached the speech detector and the recognizer at a very low level. Dictation now applies an automatic software gain before detection and recognition: soft speech is raised up to 8× (+18 dB), peaks are brought towards half of full scale, loud speech is never attenuated and nothing clips. After a loud word the gain comes back within a few seconds, so the noise between words is not pumped up.
- The bubble level follows the amplified sound, so a soft voice now moves it.
- The meeting mode is unchanged (its recording stays the raw microphone sound).

Checked by host tests only; to be confirmed on the phone: a soft voice close to the microphone, then a normal voice, then a loud one, in the same dictation.

## Installation

Installs over the previous version and keeps the models, recordings, history and statistics.
