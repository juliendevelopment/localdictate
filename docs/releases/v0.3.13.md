# LocalDictate v0.3.13 — last-minute warning on the bubble

## Dictation

- **The bubble warns before the 15-minute limit.** From 14:00 of dictation the pill turns red and shows « Arrêt automatique dans 0:59 • texte conservé », counting down every second.
- **At 15:00 the dictation ends by itself, exactly as with ✓.** The bubble shows « 15 minutes atteintes • micro coupé, texte en traitement »; the text is saved in the last dictated texts, then inserted at the cursor, or copied to the clipboard (« Non inséré • texte copié, collez-le ») if it cannot be inserted. The message in the app starts with « Limite de 15 minutes atteinte. ». Nothing is lost: this stop was already a normal end of dictation, it is now announced.

Checked by host tests only; to be confirmed on the phone.

## Installation

Installs over the previous version and keeps the models, recordings, history, statistics and « Mes mots ».
