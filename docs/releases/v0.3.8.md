# LocalDictate v0.3.8 — microphone opened at once, diagnostic line, clearer meetings

## Dictation

- The microphone now opens as soon as you tap the bubble. Before, the app first looked up the target field, paused the media, and saved a recovery file, and only then opened the microphone; the beginning of a phrase spoken right away could be missed. These steps now happen while the microphone starts.
- A long dictation could stop with « Capture saturée » after a short slowdown of the phone: the app kept well under a second of sound in reserve, not the 4 seconds intended. It now keeps 5 seconds, measured in audio time.
- After each dictation, a « Diagnostic : … » line under « Durée capturée » shows only numbers: how long after the tap the first sound arrived, how loud the voice was, the gain applied, how many pieces of speech were recognized and how long recognition took. It contains no text, stays on the screen until the next dictation and is never saved. It tells whether a missing word was lost by the microphone, by the speech detector or by the recognizer.
- The screen is refreshed once per second while dictating instead of on every piece of sound.

## Meeting

- A transcription that ends « annulé » now says why: cancelled from the app or from the notification, stopped by Android, Android's time limit, or the 6-hour processing limit.
- States are shown in French (« terminé », « audio sauvegardé, prêt pour traitement ») and durations as minutes and hours (« 1 h 03 min »), transcript positions as « 4:07 ».

Checked by host tests only; to be confirmed on the phone (see the next phone session in the status page).

## Installation

Installs over the previous version and keeps the models, recordings, history and statistics.
