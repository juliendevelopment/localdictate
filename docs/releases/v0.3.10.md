# LocalDictate v0.3.10 — last words no longer cut

## Stop delay

- Tapping ✓ right after the last word sometimes cut it. After ✓ the microphone now stays open a little longer, **0.5 s by default**, then stops; the bubble and the notification show « Fin de la phrase : micro encore ouvert… » meanwhile.
- Adjustable from 0 to 2 s in the new « Réglages » section of the Dictée screen (« Délai avant l’arrêt du micro »). Saved on the phone only.
- ✕ instead of ✓ still stops at once; disarming and the 15-minute limit too. Music resumes only once the microphone is closed.

Checked by host tests only; to be confirmed on the phone (see the next phone session in the status page).

## Installation

Installs over the previous version and keeps the models, recordings, history, statistics and « Mes mots ».
