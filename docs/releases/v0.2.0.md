# LocalDictate v0.2.0 — new bubble

## Bubble

- At rest: a small rounded square with a waveform icon, docked to the right or left edge (it snaps back to the nearest edge after dragging). Tap it to dictate.
- While dictating: a **✕ | level | ✓** pill. The level follows the real microphone input; dots are shown until the microphone delivers audio. **✓** (or tapping the level) stops and inserts at the cursor. **✕** cancels: nothing is inserted or copied, but the text is still kept in « Derniers textes dictés ».
- After a dictation: a short notice under the bubble if the text was not inserted (« Non inséré • texte copié, collez-le »), hidden after 5 s. An amber « ! » square if a text is waiting in the app.
- Hide: drag the bubble onto the **✕** that appears at the bottom of the screen (outside a dictation). The service stays armed, microphone closed. The bubble comes back when the phone is woken or unlocked, from the « Afficher la bulle » notification action, or from the button of the same name in the app.
- The bubble never takes focus: the target field keeps its cursor.

## Other

- The notification is no longer updated several times per second during dictation (only when its text changes).
- Includes the audio fixes of v0.1.9 (microphone opened on tap, widened segments).

## Installation

Same procedure as v0.1.0; installs over the previous version and keeps the models. Rendering checked in tests (Robolectric), not yet on the phone.
