# LocalDictate v0.3.1 — long spoken notes: fuller transcript, plain-text copy

## Changes (« Réunion » tab)

- **Fewer broken sentences**: speech separated by short pauses (up to 3 s) is now transcribed together, in blocks of up to 20 s, so the model keeps the context of the sentence. This should reduce fragments such as « Au niveau » / « de la machine », poor punctuation and hesitations turned into English words (« Just », « One », « Uh »). Longer pauses still separate paragraphs.
- **« Copier la transcription (texte brut) »**: copies the whole transcript as plain text, without timestamps or summary, ready to paste into another app (an LLM, notes). Copied as sensitive content; you paste it yourself.
- **Summary optional**: off by default; tick « Ajouter un résumé extractif » to get it.
- **« Retranscrire »** on a finished recording: transcribes the saved audio again with the new settings; the previous transcript is kept on disk.
- Language is detected automatically (French and English, including English terms in French; Dutch is supported by the model).

Checked by host tests only. The improvement in recognition quality is **to be confirmed on the phone** by re-transcribing the first recording.

## Installation

Installs over the previous version and keeps the models, recordings, history and statistics.
