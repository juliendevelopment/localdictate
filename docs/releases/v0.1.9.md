# LocalDictate v0.1.9 — clipped sentence starts and missing words

## Observed (Pixel, v0.1.8)

Insertion works, including several dictations in a row and in another app. But the start of sentences is cut when speaking right after tapping « Dicter », and words sometimes disappear between two sentences.

## Causes and changes

- The microphone only opened after the journal write and the loading of the Silero speech detector: the first words were never recorded. The microphone now opens immediately; Silero loads in parallel while audio waits in the queue. The bubble shows « ◌ Micro… » until the first real audio, then « ■ Arrêter ».
- The detector ended each segment exactly where silence started and kept only detected speech: soft word endings and the first syllable after a pause were lost. Each segment is now widened by 0.3 s before and after with the real captured audio, without overlap (no duplicated words).
- Continuous speech was force-cut every 8 s, sometimes inside a word: the limit is now 20 s.

## Installation

Same procedure as v0.1.0; installs over the previous version and keeps the models. To try: speak as soon as « ■ Arrêter » shows, chained sentences without pauses, a dictation longer than 20 s.
