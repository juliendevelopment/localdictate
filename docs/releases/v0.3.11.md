# LocalDictate v0.3.11 — meeting: separate stop and transcription, gain, level bars

## Meeting

- **« Arrêter l’enregistrement » no longer starts the transcription.** The meeting is saved as « audio sauvegardé », and you tap « Transcrire » when you choose. You can stop a recording and start a second one right away (for example the two halves of a long class), then transcribe both later, at home with the phone charging.
- **Gain at transcription.** New « Réglages réunion » section: automatic gain up to « Aucun » (default), +6, +12 or +18 dB. It raises a distant or soft voice without ever saturating. The recorded sound is not changed, so « Retranscrire » can try the same meeting with another setting. The transcription message names the gain used.
- **Level bars while recording**, drawn like the dictation bubble, to check that the speaker is picked up.
- Switching between Dictée and Réunion now opens the tab at its top.

Checked by host tests only; to be confirmed on the phone.

## Installation

Installs over the previous version and keeps the models, recordings, history, statistics and « Mes mots ».
