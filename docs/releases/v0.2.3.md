# LocalDictate v0.2.3 — weekly statistics

## Change

- New « Statistiques par semaine » section under the history: words dictated, dictated time (captured audio) and number of dictations for each week starting Monday, the last 8 weeks shown. Every dictation with recognized text counts, whether inserted, copied, cancelled or interrupted.
- Only the weekly totals are stored, in the app's private storage (at most 52 weeks): no text, app or time of day. « Effacer les statistiques » resets them without touching the history.

## Installation

Installs over the previous version and keeps the models. See the v0.2.1 notes for a first installation.
