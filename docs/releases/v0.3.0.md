# LocalDictate v0.3.0 — meeting mode (not yet tested on a phone)

## New

- « Réunion » tab: records up to 2 hours directly to the phone's private storage, then, after stop, transcribes locally with the same engine as dictation and builds an **extractive** summary (original sentences with their times, no rephrasing, no decisions or actions invented).
- Recording and processing run in a foreground service with a notification and a stop/cancel action. Dictation and a meeting cannot run at the same time.
- Saved meetings can be opened (transcript and summary), exported as Markdown or WAV through the share sheet, and deleted explicitly. Nothing is pasted into another app, nothing leaves the phone unless you export it.
- Starting a meeting requires prepared models, microphone and notification permissions, and about 300 MB of free space. Participants' consent is the user's responsibility.

Checked by host tests only (JVM/Robolectric, lint, builds). Recording endurance with the screen off, transcription time, memory, heat and battery are **not verified on a phone yet**. Dictation is unchanged.

## Installation

Installs over the previous version and keeps the models, history and statistics. See the v0.2.1 notes for a first installation.
