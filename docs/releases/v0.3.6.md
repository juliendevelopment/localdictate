# LocalDictate v0.3.6 — media paused while dictating

## Feature

- When music, a video or a podcast is playing, tapping the bubble to dictate pauses it, and it resumes when the microphone closes (✓, ✕, limit or disarm). LocalDictate holds a transient audio focus for the duration of the capture; players that honour audio focus pause and resume by themselves, and nothing is started if nothing was playing. A player that ignores audio focus keeps playing.

Compiled and unit-tested on the host only; to be confirmed on the phone with several players.

## Installation

Installs over the previous version and keeps the models, recordings, history and statistics.
