# LocalDictate v0.3.4 — long meetings no longer stop after an hour

## Fix

- A real meeting stopped recording after about an hour (« File d’écriture saturée : micro fermé »). The app kept less than a second of audio waiting for the disk and saved it in very small pieces; one slow moment of the storage was enough to close the microphone. It now keeps up to 60 seconds of audio in reserve and saves about one second at a time, which is also lighter on the phone.
- If a recording ever stops without you pressing stop (storage blocked, microphone taken by a call, 2-hour limit…), a notification with sound alerts you, so you can start a new recording for the rest of the meeting.

The audio already recorded is never lost: a meeting cut at 1 h 03 keeps its 1 h 03 and can be transcribed with « Retranscrire ».

Checked by host tests only, including the new capture tests; to be confirmed on the phone with a full-length meeting.

## Installation

Installs over the previous version and keeps the models, recordings, history and statistics. Includes the v0.3.2 and v0.3.3 changes.
