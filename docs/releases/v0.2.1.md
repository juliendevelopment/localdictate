# LocalDictate v0.2.1 — first public release

On-device French/English dictation for Android: tap the bubble, speak, and the text is typed at the cursor of the app you are in. Speech recognition runs entirely on the phone (NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 via sherpa-onnx, Silero VAD). No account, no cloud, no analytics.

Same app as v0.2.0, now under the Apache License 2.0, with English documentation. If you already run v0.2.0, this version installs over it and keeps the models.

## Requirements

- An arm64 Android phone. Direct insertion at the cursor needs Android 13 or later; on Android 10–12 the text is copied to the clipboard instead.
- About 488 MB to download once for the models and 1.5 GB of free space.
- The app's interface is in French.

## Install

1. Download `LocalDictate-v0.2.1.apk` below and open it. If Google Play Protect blocks it ("App blocked to protect your device"), open Play Store → profile picture → Play Protect → settings gear, turn scanning off while installing, then turn it back on.
2. Open LocalDictate, tap « Préparer » and wait for « Modèles prêts ».
3. Grant microphone, notifications, « Bulle au-dessus des apps » and « Insertion par accessibilité ». If Android says "App was denied access" for accessibility: App info → ⋮ menu → **Allow restricted settings**, then enable the service again.
4. Tap « Armer la bulle ». A small square appears at the edge of the screen.

## Use

- Put the cursor in a text field of any app, tap the bubble, wait until the level bars move, then speak. Pauses do not stop the dictation (up to 15 minutes).
- **✓** inserts the text at the cursor; **✕** cancels (the text is still kept in the app).
- If the text cannot be inserted, it is copied to the clipboard: long press → Paste. The last 5 dictations are always listed in the app.
- Drag the bubble onto the ✕ at the bottom of the screen to hide it; it comes back when you wake or unlock the phone.

## Known limitations

- Wait about a second after tapping the bubble before speaking, or the first word may be missed.
- Some transcription errors remain; speaking close to the microphone helps.
- Tested on a Pixel with Google Keep and a messaging app; other apps, web fields, English quality and battery use are not verified yet. The meeting mode is not available.
